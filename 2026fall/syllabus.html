Course Description

This is an introductory course in financial accounting. The focus is on accounting as a system for reporting business activity. It includes the study of the accounting cycle, the preparation and interpretation of basic financial statements, and the study of fundamental accounting principles.

Student Learning Outcomes

Mastery of the material covered in this course is essential for all students, regardless of specialization. The principal learning outcomes are to have each student develop and demonstrate knowledge and basic skills related to:

  • Understanding the information system used by accountants, including the process and steps in the accounting cycle.
  • Identifying the effect of business transactions on the financial statements.
  • Understanding the conventions used by accountants to record transactions and the resultant limitations of accounting data.
  • Preparing and interpreting basic financial statements.
  • Identifying the characteristics of a good accounting system, including appropriate internal controls for a business.

Using the knowledge and skills listed above, students will be able to:

  • Construct solutions for problems in business, guided by ethical principles.
  • Integrate appropriate principles and concepts to inform business decision-making.
  • Apply appropriate knowledge of technical accounting skills.
  • Integrate current technologies in summarizing and communicating accounting information.
