## 6. fig9-6.png (ddb-plug-number)
**Used in:** Section 8 — The Final-Year "Plug" Number (Figure 9-6)
**Purpose:** Visual showing why the DDB method needs a plug number in the final year

**Prompt:**
Create a professional educational diagram for a university accounting textbook showing why the DDB method needs a plug number in the final year.

Show a declining staircase/bar chart of book values over 5 years:
- Year 0 (start): $41,000 (full bar)
- End Year 1: $24,600 (after $16,400 depreciation)
- End Year 2: $14,760 (after $9,840)
- End Year 3: $8,856 (after $5,904)
- End Year 4: $5,314 (after $3,542)

At Year 5, show TWO paths branching from $5,314:
**Path A (crossed out with red X):**
- "Formula: $5,314 × 40% = $2,125.60"
- "Book Value would be $3,188.40"
- Red warning: "Above residual value — but overshoots in some scenarios"

**Path B (green checkmark):**
- "Plug: $5,314 − $1,000 = $4,314"
- "Book Value = $1,000 = Residual Value ✓"
- Green label: "CORRECT — use the plug number"

A dashed horizontal line at $1,000 labeled "Residual Value Floor — never depreciate below this"

Style: White background, staircase chart in navy blue, red/green for the two paths. Sans-serif font. 900×500px.
