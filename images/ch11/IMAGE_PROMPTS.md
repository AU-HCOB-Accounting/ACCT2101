## 4. fig11-4.png (contingency-decision)
**Used in:** Section 4 — Contingent Liabilities (Figure 11-4)
**Purpose:** Decision flowchart for determining how to report a contingent liability

**Prompt:**
Create a professional educational decision flowchart for a university accounting textbook showing how to determine the reporting treatment for a contingent liability.

**Start box (top center):**
- "Contingent Liability Exists"

**Decision 1 — "What is the likelihood?"**
- Three branches:

**Branch 1 — "Remote" (green #4caf50):**
- Result: "Do Nothing"
- No accrual, no disclosure

**Branch 2 — "Reasonably Possible" (orange #ff9800):**
- Result: "Disclose in Notes Only"
- Describe situation in notes to financial statements
- Do not record a journal entry

**Branch 3 — "Probable" (red #e53935):**
- Leads to Decision 2: "Can the amount be estimated?"
  - **No:** "Disclose in Notes Only" (describe in notes, no accrual)
  - **Yes:** "ACCRUE: Record Journal Entry AND Disclose in Notes"
    - Show entry: "Dr. Loss / Cr. Liability"
    - This is the only scenario requiring a journal entry
    - Highlight this outcome with a bold border

Style: Navy blue (#1a3a5c) primary, white background. Use traffic-light colors (green/orange/red) for the three likelihood levels. Clean diamond shapes for decisions, rounded rectangles for outcomes. Sans-serif font. 900×500px landscape.
