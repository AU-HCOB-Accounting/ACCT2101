## 8. fig8-8.png (financial-ratios)
**Used in:** Section 9 — Financial Ratios (Figure 8-8)
**Purpose:** Visual summary of the three financial ratios

**Prompt:**
Create a professional educational infographic for a university accounting textbook showing financial ratios for evaluating receivables. Show three ratio cards in a horizontal row:

**Card 1 — Acid-Test (Quick) Ratio:**
- Icon: A shield or gauge icon
- Formula: (Cash + Short-Term Investments + Net Current Receivables) ÷ Current Liabilities
- Benchmark: "≥ 1.00 is generally safe"
- Brief note: "More stringent than current ratio — excludes inventory & prepaids"

**Card 2 — A/R Turnover Ratio:**
- Icon: A circular arrow/refresh icon
- Formula: Net Credit Sales ÷ Average Net A/R
- Interpretation: "Higher = faster collections"
- Brief note: "Measures how many times A/R is collected per year"

**Card 3 — Days' Sales in Receivables:**
- Icon: A calendar icon
- Formula: 365 ÷ A/R Turnover
- Interpretation: "Lower = better"
- Brief note: "Average days to collect; also called collection period"

Card 2 and Card 3 should be connected by a small arrow showing the relationship: A/R Turnover feeds into Days' Sales calculation. Card 1 (Acid-Test) stands alone — no arrow connecting it to Card 2.

Style: Blue color scheme (#1a3a5c primary, #2b579a secondary). White background. Clean cards with subtle shadows. Sans-serif font. 860px wide, ~300px tall.
