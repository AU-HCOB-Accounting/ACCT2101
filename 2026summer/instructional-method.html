Instructional Method

Summer 2026 Instructional Method

Principles of Accounting I · Fully Online

ACCT2101CO1: Principles of Accounting I · Summer 2026

D2L (https://lms.augusta.edu/)

You will access D2L to download Lecture PPTs and watch the video lectures, found under the "Online Lectures with Lecture PPT" tab. Each chapter is a submodule containing the Lecture Video and the corresponding PPT slides. The video lectures were recorded using a previous edition of the textbook, but the posted lecture PPT slides reflect the current edition. All quizzes, exams, and assignments are based on topics covered in the Lecture Videos.

How to study: work from the Interactive Online Study Guide, which is written from these same lectures and covers the whole course. Each heading in it links straight into the lecture video at that topic, so you can read a chapter and then watch exactly the parts you need. Watching every lecture in full is still the most thorough approach and is recommended if you have the time; if you do not, read the study guide chapter first and use it to find the topics you could not follow on your own.

(Optional) Lectures from the previous in-person class are also uploaded (D2L → Content → Table of Contents → (Optional) Lectures From the Previous In-Person Class). Each begins by summarizing the important topics in the chapter and then shows how to solve some exercise problems.

Quizzes are administered online via D2L, each with a strict 10-minute time limit (see the Course Schedule for quiz due dates). Each chapter quiz consists of 5 multiple-choice questions worth 2 points each (10 points per quiz). Twelve chapters are covered, for a maximum of 120 possible quiz points; however, your two lowest quiz scores are dropped, making the quiz total counted toward your final grade 100 points.

MyAccountingLab

Graded Assignments: Complete each chapter's assignment in MyAccountingLab (Assignment → Do Homework). Graded assignments consist of "Quick Check," "Short Exercises," and "Exercises" problems from the end of each chapter. You have unlimited attempts on each problem. Late submissions receive a 50% penalty. (Late submissions are not allowed for the Ch 9, 11, 12, and 13 graded assignments — see the Course Schedule.)

Exams: Exams are taken online through MyAccountingLab on the dates listed in the Course Schedule. If you miss a test or the final exam, you receive a zero for that exam (except for documented emergencies: serious illness, family medical emergency, or a university-sponsored activity). Documentation must be provided before the test or as soon as possible; non-emergency excused cases must be communicated ahead of time. Job-related activities, travel plans, lack of a vehicle or child care, and non-university activities are not acceptable excuses. A make-up test taken after the scheduled time will be more difficult and will not be curved, to be fair to students who took it on time.

  • Instructions for the "Ch1-2-practice-test" are provided by email.
  • Exams #1 and #2 consist of 60 multiple-choice questions.
  • Exam #3 and the Comprehensive Final Exam consist of problems from the MyAccountingLab Chapter Graded Assignments.
  • All exams (including quizzes) are timed. You are given 120 minutes to complete each exam. You may start at any time during the designated exam date but must finish within the time limit. If you experience technical difficulties, notify the instructor immediately.

Other study materials on MyAccountingLab (sample quizzes/tests, the Study Plan, Dynamic Study Modules) are provided by the publisher and may include topics not covered in class. Use them only after fully reviewing the lecture materials and completing your required assignments.

Communication Rule

The instructor communicates via email and D2L discussion boards. Email first; if you post on the discussion boards, also notify the instructor by email. You are encouraged to post questions so other students can see the responses. Before asking any question by email, students must first have watched either (1) the chapter video lectures or (2) the optional in-person recordings. The instructor can verify viewing activity through the system's access records and will respond to students who have reviewed the relevant lecture content. Confirmed viewers who still have questions may request a 1-on-1 MS Teams meeting.

See also the Syllabus and Course Schedule for Summer 2026.
