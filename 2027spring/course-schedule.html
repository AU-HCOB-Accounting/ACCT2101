Full 15-week Spring semester (28 sessions). Chapters, assignment/quiz due dates, and exam dates for ACCT2101CO1 (CRN 24076) — Spring 2027. This schedule stretches the same twelve-chapter, four-exam sequence across the full term.

Important Notes

Quizzes are delivered via D2L; all Graded Assignments are delivered via MyAccountingLab. The video lectures were recorded using a previous edition of the textbook, but the posted lecture PPT slides reflect the current (8th) edition. All quizzes, exams, and assignments are based on topics covered in the Lecture Videos, so watching the lecture videos first is highly recommended.

No late submissions are accepted — for any assignment or quiz. Anything submitted after 11:59 PM on its due date receives a zero; there is no partial credit for late work.
